# For the machine

You are running on a computer with unknown hardware attached. Nobody will tell you what it is.

## Your task

Identify, integrate, and iterate: build a human-usable dashboard of this machine and its attachments, presented on the desktop GUI.

## Rules

- No documentation exists. Discover everything yourself.
- Keep a diary in this repository: every hypothesis, test, result, and dead end, committed as you go. The diary matters as much as the dashboard.
- You may ask a human for help **only** if you have strong evidence something is physically broken — a device that never powers, wiring that cannot work. Everything else is your problem.
- Do not damage the body you are exploring. When unsure whether an action is destructive, find a safer probe first.
